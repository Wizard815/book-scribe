Atlanta campaign
The Atlanta campaign was a series of battles fought in northwest Georgia and around Atlanta during the summer of 1864. Union Major General William T. Sherman advanced from Chattanooga in May 1864 against the Confederate Army of Tennessee. After months of marching and fighting, including battles at Resaca, Kennesaw Mountain, Peachtree Creek and Atlanta, Sherman's armies entered Atlanta on 2 September 1864. The fall of Atlanta helped President Abraham Lincoln win re-election in November 1864.
