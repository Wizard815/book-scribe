Grand Review of the Armies
The Grand Review of the Armies was a military parade in Washington, D.C., on 23 and 24 May 1865, after the end of the American Civil War. On the first day the Army of the Potomac marched down Pennsylvania Avenue; on the second day Sherman's armies of the West marched, watched by President Andrew Johnson and crowds of thousands. Soon after, most volunteer regiments were sent home and mustered out.
