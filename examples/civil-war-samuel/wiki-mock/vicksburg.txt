Siege of Vicksburg
The Siege of Vicksburg, from 18 May to 4 July 1863, was the final major action of the Vicksburg campaign. Union Major General Ulysses S. Grant's Army of the Tennessee surrounded the Confederate fortress city of Vicksburg, Mississippi, on the Mississippi River. After two failed assaults Grant laid siege. The Confederate commander, Lieutenant General John C. Pemberton, surrendered on 4 July 1863. Together with the Union victory at Gettysburg the day before, the fall of Vicksburg is seen as a turning point of the war; it gave the Union control of the Mississippi River.
