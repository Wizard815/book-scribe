76th Ohio Infantry Regiment
The 76th Ohio Infantry Regiment was an infantry regiment of the Union Army during the American Civil War. It was organized at Camp Sherman in Newark, Ohio, in the winter of 1861 and 1862 and mustered in on 9 February 1862 for three years, under Colonel Charles R. Woods. Most of its men came from Licking County and the counties around it.

The regiment fought at Fort Donelson in February 1862 and was at Shiloh and the advance on Corinth. In 1863 it served in the Vicksburg campaign, then moved to Chattanooga and fought at Lookout Mountain and Missionary Ridge in November 1863. At Ringgold Gap, Georgia, on 27 November 1863 it attacked across open ground against Confederate troops on the ridges and lost heavily; its colors were shot down several times.

In 1864 the regiment marched in the Atlanta campaign, then with General William T. Sherman on the March to the Sea and through the Carolinas in 1865. It marched in the Grand Review in Washington in May 1865 and was mustered out at Louisville, Kentucky, in July 1865.
