Sherman's March to the Sea
Sherman's March to the Sea was a military campaign from 15 November to 21 December 1864, from Atlanta to the port of Savannah, Georgia. About 62,000 Union soldiers marched some 285 miles in two wings, living off the land, destroying railroads, factories and supplies. Savannah was taken on 21 December 1864, and Sherman offered the city to Lincoln as a Christmas present. In early 1865 the armies marched north through the Carolinas.
