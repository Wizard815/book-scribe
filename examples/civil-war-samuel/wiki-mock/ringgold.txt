Battle of Ringgold Gap
The Battle of Ringgold Gap was fought on 27 November 1863 near Ringgold, Georgia, at the end of the Chattanooga campaign. Confederate Major General Patrick Cleburne's division held the narrow gap in Taylor's Ridge, where the railroad and East Chickamauga Creek pass through, to protect the retreat of the Army of Tennessee and its wagons.

Union troops under Major General Joseph Hooker attacked the gap and the ridges on both sides through the morning. The Confederates held for about five hours and then withdrew. Union losses were about 500 killed and wounded, Confederate losses about 220. The 76th Ohio, which attacked across open ground, was among the regiments that suffered most.
