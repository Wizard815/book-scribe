Dependent and Disability Pension Act
The Dependent and Disability Pension Act of 27 June 1890 gave pensions to Union veterans of the Civil War who had served at least 90 days, had been honorably discharged, and were unable to do manual labor, whether or not the disability came from their service. Veterans applied with a sworn declaration before a county official, with witnesses. By 1893 nearly a million veterans and widows were on the pension rolls.
