Newark, Ohio
Newark is a city in and the county seat of Licking County, Ohio, about 30 miles east of Columbus. It was founded in 1802. The Ohio and Erie Canal reached Newark in the 1820s and the railroad in the 1850s, and the town grew as a market for the farms around it. During the Civil War, Camp Sherman near Newark was used to organize volunteer regiments, including the 76th Ohio Infantry. Cedar Hill Cemetery, opened in 1850, holds the graves of many Civil War veterans.
