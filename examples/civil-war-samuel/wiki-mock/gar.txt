Grand Army of the Republic
The Grand Army of the Republic (G.A.R.) was a fraternal organization of Union veterans of the Civil War, founded in Decatur, Illinois, in 1866. It had local posts in towns across the North, held reunions and encampments, cared for veterans' widows and orphans, and pressed Congress for pensions. It helped establish Memorial Day, first observed nationally on 30 May 1868. At its peak in 1890 it had about 410,000 members.
