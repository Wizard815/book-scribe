---
name: Everett Lund
---
Born 1896 on a wheat farm near Lindsborg, Kansas, to Swedish parents. Joined the Kansas National Guard and the army in 1917, trained at Camp Doniphan in Oklahoma, sailed to France with the 35th Division in 1918, fought in the Meuse-Argonne in September 1918. Kept a pressed prairie flower in his Bible and a postcard from the French village of Charpentry. Came home in spring 1919 and farmed the same land until 1971.
