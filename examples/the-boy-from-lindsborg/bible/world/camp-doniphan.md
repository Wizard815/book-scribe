---
name: Camp Doniphan
---
US Army training camp at Fort Sill, Oklahoma, established in 1917 for the 35th Division. Tents and hastily built barracks on the prairie; hot and dusty summer of 1917, cold windy winter. Men drilled with wooden rifles until real ones arrived, and learned trench digging, bayonet work and artillery cooperation. Closed in 1919.
