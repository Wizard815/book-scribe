---
name: 35th Division
---
Formed in 1917 from National Guard units of Missouri and Kansas; trained at Camp Doniphan, Fort Sill, Oklahoma; sailed for France in spring 1918; trained in the Vosges; entered the Meuse-Argonne Offensive on 26 September 1918, fighting near Vauquois and Exermont. Captain Harry S. Truman commanded Battery D of the 129th Field Artillery.
