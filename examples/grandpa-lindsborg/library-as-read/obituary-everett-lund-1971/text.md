Lindsborg News-Record, March 1971 (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
EVERETT N. LUND, 74, FARMER AND VETERAN
Everett Nils Lund, born 9 March 1896 on the family farm near Lindsborg,
died at home on 2 March 1971. He served in the 35th Division in France in
1918 and was in the Meuse-Argonne offensive. After the war he returned to
the farm and worked the same land for more than fifty years.
He is survived by his wife Elsa; his brother Olof; two sons; and four
grandchildren. Services will be at Messiah Lutheran Church, Lindsborg.
He was known for his pressed-flower collection and his habit of never
talking about the war.
