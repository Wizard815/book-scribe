Letter from Everett to his mother (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
Somewhere in France, 14 October 1918
Dear Mother,
I am well and I hope this finds you and Father the same. We have been
a long time in the mud and the rain, and I have not had a dry sock since
the end of September. I cannot tell you where we are, but I can tell you
the village on the postcard I send with this is called Charpentry. It was
a pretty place once. Do not worry about the wheat; Olof will manage it.
The flu is going through the camps and some of the boys have gone to the
hospital. I have kept the flower you pressed in my Bible and I read the
psalm you marked every night. Tell Father I did not forget what he said
at the station.
Your loving son,
Everett
