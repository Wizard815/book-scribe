---
name: continuity-check
description: Check a passage or an idea against what the book and the wiki already say
---
1. Use search_book and bible_lookup for each person, place and date the passage touches.
2. Compare. Name any clash plainly: what the passage says, what the book says, and where (show_passage on the old line).
3. If nothing clashes, say so in one sentence. Do not invent problems.
4. Offer the smallest fix, and wait for the writer to choose.
