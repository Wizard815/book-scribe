---
name: scene-from-memory
description: Build a scene from something the writer said or described long ago
---
1. Use recall with the writer's own words to find what they said, and search_library for related notes or photos.
2. Quote the writer's original wording back to them briefly, with the date, so they know you found it.
3. Ask which parts they want kept exactly. Then draft from those details, adding at most one invented detail (take it from random_ideas kind sense).
