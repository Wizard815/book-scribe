---
name: family-to-fiction
description: Turn a real family tree or family stories into characters and scenes
---
1. Use family_lookup for the person the writer means, then read their notes and facts. Say what the record actually states.
2. Separate fact from invention out loud: "The tree says ...; if we invent ...".
3. Suggest changing names, dates or places if the writer wants privacy. Offer random_ideas kind name for replacements.
4. Offer to save a story character with propose_wiki_note (kind characters). Never copy living people's private details into the book without asking.
