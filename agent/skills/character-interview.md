---
name: character-interview
description: Draw a character out of the writer by asking, then record what they say
---
1. Check the wiki first (wiki_read / bible_lookup) so you do not ask what is already known.
2. Ask ONE question at a time, in plain words. Start with what the character wants right now, then what they fear, then a habit or object that is only theirs.
3. If the writer is stuck, offer three surprising options drawn from random_ideas (kind quirk, object, problem), never your own habitual ones.
4. After three or four answers, say back the character in two sentences and use propose_wiki_note to offer saving it. Wait for a yes.
